Our Story

Company Profile

Rasambari is the confluence of creative brilliance and aesthetic quotient in the form of handmade organic products to enhance your skin's health and overall wellness, enabling you to shine in your own unique way.

We manufacture handmade organic, vegan and natural soaps, bath salts, shampoos, hair oils, lotions, moisturizers, body oils, creams, body butter, butter soaps, lip balms and jewellery.

Each and every product is handmade and curated to assure the wellness of our consumers.

All ingredients used to make our products are natural, organic, chemical-free, vegan and cruelty-free, alcohol-free, SLS-free, SLES-free and paraben-free.
